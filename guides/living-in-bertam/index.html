Living in Bertam, Kepala Batas, Penang

Bertam is the fast-developing side of Kepala Batas, the seat of northern Seberang Perai on the Penang mainland. Schools, a public hospital, malls and two big lake parks sit within about ten minutes of each other. Penang Technology Park @ Bertam, where new factories are opening, is about 5 minutes from Panthera Bertam.

Updated 13 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

Where Bertam and Kepala Batas are

The chain, smallest first: Bertam, Kepala Batas, Seberang Perai Utara (northern Seberang Perai), Penang mainland, Pulau Pinang. Postcode 13200. This is Kepala Batas, Penang, not the town of the same name in Kedah.

Kepala Batas is the seat of the Seberang Perai Utara district, and the local council is the Seberang Perai City Council (MBSP).[1][7] The district had 339,095 people in the 2020 census.[2]

The name is Malay: kepala is the head or front end of something, and batas the earth dykes around a paddy field.[1] It stayed a rice-growing town for a long time. Today it is known as an administrative and education centre for the north, and as the hometown of Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi, Malaysia's 5th Prime Minister.[3]

Bertam sits close to the North-South Expressway.[4] It was mostly estates, kampung and farmland, and is now a fast-developing township.[4] The Star puts it about 2 km from central Kepala Batas, a busy area where families come out in the evenings to shop and eat.[5] A reminder of the old days still stands: Bertam House, built in 1913 as the estate manager's bungalow of the Bertam rubber estate, whose land later became the Bertam Perdana township.[6]

  • By car. Bertam has its own interchange on the North-South Expressway, Exit 166. It links to Jalan Tun Hamdan Sheikh Tahir, the main road through Bertam, Kepala Batas, Tikam Batu and Tasek Gelugor. Heading south, the next exits lead to Butterworth, Seberang Jaya and the Penang Bridge.[21]
  • By train. The nearest station is Tasek Gelugor, on the KTM Komuter Padang Besar to Butterworth line, with Sungai Petani and Bukit Mertajam as the next stops.[22] Check times with KTMB before you travel.
  • By bus. Rapid Penang route 601 runs between Penang Sentral and Kepala Batas.[1]

Schools and colleges

Bertam is thick with schools, from primary to university. The two closest are right next to the estate.

  • SK Bertam Perdana (primary) and SMK Bertam Perdana (secondary), two separate schools side by side, about 0.5 to 0.6 km by road from the estate.[18]
  • SM Sains Kepala Batas, a fully residential science school, about 5 minutes.[17]
  • SMK Dato' Kailan and MRSM Kepala Batas, each within about 1.3 km in a straight line.[18]
  • UiTM Kampus Bertam, a university campus, about 6 minutes.[18]
  • Kolej Matrikulasi Pulau Pinang, the pre-university college, about 13 minutes.[18]
  • GiatMARA Kepala Batas, Kolej Kejururawatan Bertam (nursing) and Institut Latihan Perindustrian Kepala Batas (industrial training), all within about 2 km in a straight line.[18]

Hospitals and clinics

  • Hospital Kepala Batas, the government hospital, about 9 minutes. It is part of the Seberang Perai hospital cluster led by Seberang Jaya Hospital.[23]
  • Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi USM, formerly the Advanced Medical and Dental Institute (AMDI), renamed in January 2026. A cancer teaching hospital in Bertam with medical and dental specialists, about 7 minutes.[24]
  • Klinik Desa Paya Keladi, a community clinic, about 6 minutes.

Shopping, food and daily needs

  • Sunshine Bertam, a mall in the Bertam Perdana township, opened in 2013 around a Sunshine hypermarket anchor (The Star, Apr 2013). About 6 minutes.[13]
  • Mydin Bertam Mall, a hypermarket in Bandar Putra Bertam on Jalan Tun Hamdan Sheikh Tahir. About 6 minutes.[14]
  • Lotus's hypermarket, about 8 minutes, and Kepala Batas town, with its banks and shops, also about 8 minutes.
  • Bertam Walk, Bertam Souq and the Bandar Putra Bertam hawker centre are listed by Penang Travel Tips as local spots.[3][4]

For food, Penang Travel Tips points to Malay breakfasts, kuih and hawker meals in Kepala Batas town, and lists a night market and a wet market there.[3] Restoran Ikan Bakar Din, on Jalan Butterworth in Kepala Batas, is known for its grilled fish (Time Out Penang, 2014) and is still listed in a July 2026 round-up of ikan bakar spots in Penang.[15]

Parks, sport and places of worship

Parks and recreation

  • Taman Bandaraya Vision Park, about 6 minutes. A 128-acre city park run by MBSP, with a lake, plaza, playground, exercise stations and a flower garden opened in December 2024. People use it for cycling, aerobics and events.[7]
  • Setia Fontaines Heritage Park, about 6 minutes. About 40 ha around a 25.5 ha lake, with a 4.1 km jogging track and a musical fountain that plays Friday to Sunday evenings. Open daily, 6am to midnight.[10]
  • Bertam Resort & Water Park, about 9 minutes, next to a golf course. It first opened in December 2022[11] and is due to reopen as The Bertam, Penang, run by Marriott, in Q4 2026 (as of May 2026).[12]
  • Bertam Indah sports pitches and tennis courts, public, about 4 minutes.

Places of worship

  • Masjid Abdullah Fahim, the biggest mosque in Kepala Batas, next to Vision Park.[7] Opened in 2012, it holds 6,000.[8] Closer in, Surau Bertam Perdana is about 5 minutes away.
  • Other mosques include Masjid Al-Jamiul Badawi and Masjid At-Taqwa.[3]
  • Kepala Batas Tau Bo Keong Temple (威北甲抛峇底斗母宫) on Jalan Dua, one of the main Chinese temples in town.[16]
  • Sri Maha Mariamman Temple, Kepala Batas.[3]

Further afield, within reach

  • Kulim Hi-Tech Park, Kedah, about 45 minutes. Its first four phases, 2,161 acres of industrial land, were fully leased by November 2024, with Intel, its first tenant, and investors such as AT&S.[33] In Kulim, Ferrotec opened a second plant in October 2026, with over 1,000 jobs.[34]
  • Prai Industrial Area, about 27 minutes, and Penang Port at North Butterworth, about 28 minutes.
  • Bandar Cassia Technology Park, Seberang Perai: MKS opened a plant there in June 2026 and expects over 1,000 jobs once all phases are done.[35]
  • Bayan Lepas Free Industrial Zone on the island, about 40 minutes. ASE opened its fifth Penang plant there in February 2025 and plans to hire 1,500 more staff.[36]

Across the state, Penang had RM 22.4 billion of approved manufacturing investment in 2025, from 232 projects expected to create 24,633 jobs.[37] In the first half of 2026 it drew RM 17.3 billion, the most of any state, from 177 projects expected to create 19,337 jobs.[38]

Questions about living in Bertam

Is Bertam in Penang or Kedah?

Penang. Bertam is part of Kepala Batas, the seat of the Seberang Perai Utara district on the Penang mainland, postcode 13200. There is a different Kepala Batas in Kedah.

How far is Bertam from Butterworth and the Penang Bridge?

From Panthera Bertam it is about 25 minutes to Butterworth town, 28 minutes to Penang Sentral and 30 minutes to the Penang Bridge, by approximate drive times. The NSE Bertam Interchange, Exit 166, is about 7 minutes away.

Is there a train station near Bertam?

The nearest is KTM Tasek Gelugor, about 14 minutes by road, on the KTM Komuter Padang Besar to Butterworth line.

Which schools are near Panthera Bertam?

SK Bertam Perdana and SMK Bertam Perdana are right next to the estate. SM Sains Kepala Batas is about 5 minutes away and UiTM Kampus Bertam about 6. The nearest Chinese primary school, SJK (C) Pei Yu in Kepala Batas, is about 9 minutes by road.

Is there a hospital in Kepala Batas?

Yes. Hospital Kepala Batas, the government hospital, is about 9 minutes away, and Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi USM, formerly AMDI, about 7. A 100-bed private hospital in Bertam is planned, with a 2028 target.

Where do people shop in Bertam?

Sunshine Bertam mall and Mydin Bertam are each about 6 minutes from the estate, and Lotus's and Kepala Batas town about 8 minutes.

How far is Penang Technology Park @ Bertam?

About 5 minutes by car. As of July 2026 the 880-acre park had all its infrastructure done and more than 60% of its industrial land taken up.
