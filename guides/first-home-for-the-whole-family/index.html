A first home for the whole family: the Panthera Bertam Semi-D

The Semi-D at Panthera Bertam is a freehold first home designed to grow with your family. It has 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms in 2,006 sq ft, with a ground-floor bedroom for parents that has its own door into a bathroom also reached from the living area, a private side lawn on its open side, and the shared green of over 700 m² right in front. SK Bertam Perdana is about a 7-minute walk away and SMK Bertam Perdana is next to the estate. Prices start from RM 712,500, below the RM750,000 ceiling of the first-home stamp duty relief proposed in Budget 2027, which is not yet law.

Every Semi-D has the same 2,006 sq ft built-up; what changes from lot to lot is the land around it.[1]

Room for the children

Upstairs there are three bedrooms, and each one has its own bathroom, so mornings before school are less of a queue.[1]

  • Master bedroom 337 sq ft, with its own bathroom and sliding glass doors onto a 105 sq ft front balcony
  • Bedroom 1 192 sq ft, with its own bathroom
  • Bedroom 2 156 sq ft, with its own bathroom

Downstairs, the kitchen, living and dining are one open space, with floor-to-ceiling sliding glass doors from the living and dining rooms onto your side lawn, so you can keep an eye on the children while you cook. Generous floor-to-floor heights of 3.7 m downstairs and 3.45 m upstairs make the rooms feel open and airy, and the car porch takes two cars.[1]

Room sizes are taken from the plans. Plans are indicative and subject to the approved drawings.

A ground-floor bedroom for parents

Every Semi-D has a 145 sq ft bedroom on the ground floor, beside the living, dining and kitchen, with its own door into a bathroom, which is also reached from the living area, so parents need not use the stairs.[1] Grandparents who live with you, or come to stay, can have a room downstairs, while the family sleeps upstairs. It also works as a guest room or a study until you need it.

That is the idea behind the Semi-D: a home with room for three generations, designed to grow with your family rather than one you outgrow.

A private side lawn, and the green in front

Every Semi-D comes with a private side lawn on its open side, away from the party wall it shares with its neighbour: your own patch of grass beside the house, for the children to play on.[1]

In front, every one of the 19 homes faces the shared green of over 700 m², kept as permanent public open space and a short walk from every front gate. A 40-foot inner ring road with two entrances runs around it, and the layout keeps through-traffic out. The estate is not gated.[2] More on the layout in The estate.

Schools and daily needs close by

SK Bertam Perdana, a national primary school, is across the road, about a 7-minute walk. SMK Bertam Perdana, the secondary school, sits right next to the estate.[3][2]

Freehold, with its own title

The estate is on freehold land, Lot 24480, and each home gets its own individual title at handover.[1] The 16 Semi-D lots run from 210 to 318 m² of land. Estimated completion is Q4 2028.

From RM 712,500, and the proposed Budget 2027 relief

FromRM 712,500

This is the pre-launch price, E. & O.E. Corner lots 04 and 19 carry a premium. Ask sales on WhatsApp for the live lot-by-lot list.[1]

Budget 2027, tabled on 9 October 2026, proposes stamp duty relief for Malaysian citizens buying a first home priced up to RM750,000.[4][5] That would put a freehold semi-D under RM750k in Penang, like the Semi-D at its starting price, within the proposed ceiling. It is a proposal, not yet law, as of 13 November 2026, and who qualifies depends on the final rules. Questions from families

Is the Semi-D big enough for children and grandparents?

It is designed to grow with your family. The Semi-D has 2,006 sq ft built-up, 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms: three bedrooms upstairs, each with its own bathroom, and one bedroom on the ground floor for parents or guests, with its own door into a bathroom, which is also reached from the living area.

Is there a bedroom on the ground floor?

Yes. Every Semi-D has a 145 sq ft bedroom on the ground floor, next to the living, dining and kitchen. It has its own door into a bathroom, which is also reached from the living area, so parents need not use the stairs.

Does the Semi-D have its own garden?

Yes. Every Semi-D has a private side lawn on its open side (the other side is the shared party wall), and every home in the estate faces the shared green of over 700 m² in front.

How far are the schools?

SK Bertam Perdana, a primary school, is across the road, about a 7-minute walk. SMK Bertam Perdana, a secondary school, is right next to the estate.

Is the Semi-D freehold?

Yes. The estate is on freehold land, Lot 24480, and each home gets its own individual title at handover.

Is the Semi-D priced under RM750,000?
