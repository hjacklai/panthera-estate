Panthera Bertam: a small freehold estate on the Penang mainland

Panthera Bertam is just 19 two-storey homes, 16 semi-Ds and 3 bungalows, on 2 acres of freehold land in Bertam, Kepala Batas. Every home faces a shared green of over 700 m², kept as open space, inside a 40-foot ring road with two entrances. It is in pre-launch now, with handover estimated for Q4 2028.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

The site is Lot 24480, Bertam, in Seberang Perai Utara on the Penang mainland, at about 5.5307 N, 100.4655 E.[1] The developer is Panthera Development Sdn. Bhd.[1]

The layout: homes around a green

The estate is a courtyard plan. All nineteen homes sit around a shared green of over 700 m², a short walk from every front gate, and every home faces it.[2]

  • The central green. Over 700 m² of shared open space, kept as permanent public open space.[1]
  • The ring road. A 40-foot inner ring road runs around the green, with two entrances into the taman.[2]
  • Not gated. The estate has no gate. The inner-ring layout keeps through-traffic out.[1][2]
  • Drainage built in. A 298 m² retention pond (kolam tadahan) and perimeter drains are part of the estate's design.[1][2]

Planting and landscaping shown in our renders and in the 3D walkthrough are illustrative.[1]

Two house types

All 19 homes are two-storey, in a modern tropical design: pitched terracotta roofs, light walls, floor-to-ceiling sliding glass doors to the garden, and generous floor-to-floor heights of 3.7 m downstairs and 3.45 m upstairs that keep rooms open and airy.[1] Sizes run from 2,006 to 2,642 sq ft built-up.[2]

Land and title

The site is 7,939.59 m², or 1.962 acres, about 2 acres, and it is freehold.[1] Each home comes with its own individual title at handover.[1]

Every lot is a different size. The 16 semi-D lots run from 210 to 318 m², and the three bungalow lots, numbered 01 to 03, from 356 to 493 m².[1] Sales can send the lot-by-lot list with sizes and availability.[2]

What 19 homes means

We call Panthera Bertam a boutique development.[2] In practice, that comes down to a few plain facts about this estate:

  • Only 19 homes. Sixteen semi-Ds and three bungalows, and no more.[2]
  • Every home faces the green.[2]
  • The green stays green. It is kept as permanent public open space.[1]
  • An inner ring road. The ring road serves the 19 homes, and the layout keeps through-traffic out.[1]
  • Room around each house. Land per home runs from 210 m² for the smallest semi-D lot to 493 m² for the largest bungalow lot.[1]

Looking after the estate

  • Residents' Association. From handover, a Residents' Association looks after the shared parts: the green, the roads, the drains and the pond.[1][2]
  • Changing your home later. Renovation is allowed within the approved building envelope. A porch enclosure and a rear-kitchen extension are pre-approved. Changes that alter the streetscape need sign-off from the Residents' Association and MBSP, the Seberang Perai City Council. A guideline is given at handover.[1][2]

Who is next door

  • SK Bertam Perdana, a primary school across the road, about 300 m away, a 7-minute walk.[3]
  • SMK Bertam Perdana, a secondary school, nearby.[1]
  • Established terrace houses behind the site.[1]
  • Penang Technology Park @ Bertam, about 5 minutes by car.[1]

Schools, hospitals, shops, parks and drive times further out are in our Living in Bertam guide.

To live in, or to rent out

A home here is yours to use as you choose: to live in, or to rent out. Penang Technology Park @ Bertam is about 5 minutes away, and more than 60% of its industrial land had been taken up as of July 2026.[1] Our Living in Bertam guide lists the companies building there and nearby, with dates and sources.

We do not publish rent figures or returns.

Questions about the estate

What is Panthera Bertam?

A pre-launch freehold estate of 19 two-storey homes in Bertam, Kepala Batas, on the Penang mainland: 16 semi-detached homes and 3 bungalows around a shared green of over 700 m². The developer is Panthera Development Sdn. Bhd.

Is Panthera Bertam freehold?

Yes. The whole 2-acre site is freehold, and each home gets its own individual title at handover.

Is Panthera Bertam a gated estate?

No. It is not gated. The homes sit on a 40-foot inner ring road with two entrances, and the layout keeps through-traffic out.

Who looks after the central green?

A Residents' Association looks after the green, the roads, the drains and the retention pond from handover. The green is kept as permanent public open space.

When will the homes be ready?

Pre-launch registration is open in 2026, and handover is estimated for Q4 2028.

Can I see the homes before they are built?

Yes. Our 3D walkthrough lets you scroll through each home, built from the floor plans, and out to the street and the estate. Furniture and landscaping in it are illustrative.

Can I rent my home out?

It is your home, to live in or to rent out as you choose. Penang Technology Park @ Bertam is about 5 minutes away, and our Living in Bertam guide lists the companies building there.
