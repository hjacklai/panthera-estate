The Semi-D · 16 homes

A first home in Bertam, Penang, built to grow with your family

The Semi-D at Panthera Bertam is a two-storey freehold semi-detached first home in Bertam, Kepala Batas, Seberang Perai Utara, on the Penang mainland: 2,006 sq ft built-up on 210 to 318 m² of land, 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms. Three bedrooms upstairs for the children, each with its own bathroom. A ground-floor bedroom for parents or guests. A private side lawn on the open side, and a shared green of over 700 m² in the estate. In pre-launch now, from RM 712,500.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

A first home for the whole family

Room for the children now, and for parents who may move in later. A freehold semi-D in Penang from RM 712,500, below RM750,000, with its own individual title at handover.

  • Parents on the ground floor. A 145 sq ft bedroom downstairs for parents, grandparents or guests. No stairs to climb.

The shared green

The estate has a shared green of over 700 m², kept as permanent public open space. A short walk from every front gate.

A 40-foot inner ring road runs around it, with two entrances, and the layout keeps through-traffic out. The estate is not gated. A 298 m² retention pond and perimeter drains are built in.
