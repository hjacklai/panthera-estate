The Semi-D: a new freehold semi-D in Bertam, Penang

The Semi-D at Panthera Bertam is a two-storey, freehold semi-detached home in Bertam, Kepala Batas, on the Penang mainland. It has 2,006 sq ft built up on 210 to 318 m² of land, 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms, and it faces a shared green of over 700 m². Pre-launch prices start from RM 726,000.

A big open-plan kitchen, living and dining, with high ceilings and floor-to-ceiling glass to the garden. Three bedrooms upstairs, one on the ground floor. The living room has the full 3.5 m ceiling.

Land sizes: 210 to 318 m²

The 16 semi-D lots run from 210 to 318 m² (2,260 to 3,423 sq ft). Each home has the same 2,006 sq ft built-up; what changes from lot to lot is the land around it. Across the estate, setbacks leave 49 to 201 m² of green margin per lot.

Corner lots 04 and 19 carry a corner premium. Six of the 19 lots in the estate are Bumiputera lots with a 5% discount, and those transfer only to Bumiputera owners. Sales can send the live lot-by-lot list with sizes and availability.

The green it faces

All 19 homes sit around a shared green of over 700 m², kept as permanent public open space. It is a short walk from every front gate.

A 40-foot inner ring road with two entrances runs around it, and the layout keeps through-traffic out. The estate is not gated. A 298 m² retention pond and perimeter drains are built in. A Residents' Association looks after the green, roads, drains and pond from handover, at around RM 180 per home a month.

Semi-D questions

Is the Semi-D freehold?

Yes. The estate is on freehold land, Lot 24480, and each home gets its own individual title at handover.

How much is the Semi-D?

From RM 726,000 at pre-launch, subject to the SPA. Corner lots 04 and 19 carry a premium, and Bumi lots get a 5% discount. Ask sales on WhatsApp for the live lot-by-lot list.

How many bedrooms does the Semi-D have?

Four bedrooms and four bathrooms: one bedroom on the ground floor and three upstairs, and each upstairs bedroom has its own bathroom.

Can foreigners buy the Semi-D?

Generally not. On the Penang mainland the minimum price for foreign buyers of landed homes is RM 1 million (Bar Council Circular 444/2024, dated 23 Dec 2024), and the Semi-D is priced below it. Ask sales for the current rule.

When will the Semi-D be ready?

Estimated handover is Q4 2028, 24 months from SPA signing. The date is fixed in the SPA with an LAD clause, so you are compensated if the build runs late.
