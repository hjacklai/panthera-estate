The Bungalow: a new freehold bungalow in Bertam, Penang

The Bungalow is the largest home at Panthera Bertam: a two-storey, freehold detached house for sale in Bertam, Kepala Batas, on the Penang mainland. It has 2,642 sq ft built up on 356 to 493 m² of land, 4+1 bedrooms, 5+1 bathrooms, and faces the estate's shared green. It is a new launch in pre-launch, there are only three, and each is priced by its land size, on request.

Land sizes: 356 to 493 m²

The three bungalows sit on lots 01 to 03, from 356 to 493 m² (3,832 to 5,308 sq ft). The house is the same on each; the land is not, so each bungalow is priced individually by its land size.

Across the estate, setbacks leave 49 to 201 m² of green margin per lot. Sales can tell you which bungalow lots are still open and what each one costs.

The green it faces

All 19 homes sit around a shared green of over 700 m², kept as permanent public open space. It is a short walk from every front gate.

A 40-foot inner ring road with two entrances runs around it, and the layout keeps through-traffic out. The estate is not gated. A 298 m² retention pond and perimeter drains are built in. A Residents' Association looks after the green, roads, drains and pond from handover, at around RM 180 per home a month.

Bungalow questions

Is the Bungalow freehold?

Yes. The estate is on freehold land, Lot 24480, and each home gets its own individual title at handover.

How much is the Bungalow?

The price is on request. Each of the three bungalows is priced by its land size, from 356 to 493 m². Ask sales on WhatsApp for the current figures.

How many bedrooms does the Bungalow have?

4+1: a guest bedroom and a maid's room on the ground floor, and the master bedroom plus two more bedrooms upstairs. Bathrooms are 5+1: five bathrooms plus a powder room on the ground floor.

When will the Bungalow be ready?

Estimated completion is Q4 2028.
