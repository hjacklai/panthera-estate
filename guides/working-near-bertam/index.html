Working near Bertam: jobs near Penang Technology Park @ Bertam

Penang Technology Park @ Bertam, where new factories are opening, is about 5 minutes from Panthera Bertam. INV's battery-separator plant has been running there since June 2025, and more plants are being built. Prai, Penang Port, Bayan Lepas and Kulim Hi-Tech Park are all within about 45 minutes by car.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

Penang Technology Park @ Bertam

Penang Technology Park @ Bertam (PTP @ Bertam) is an 880-acre industrial park developed by Ideal Property Group and launched in March 2023.[16] As of July 2026 all its infrastructure was complete and more than 60% of the industrial land had been taken up.[1] Five months earlier, in February 2026, Phase 1 was complete, Phase 2's core infrastructure was 85% done and take-up stood at more than 55%, including several China-based high-tech companies.[2][3]

The park offers light and medium industrial lots, commercial land and built-to-lease factories. Enquiries come from semiconductors, electrical and electronics (E&E), automotive and automotive parts, new energy, energy storage and other high-tech sectors.[1]

From Panthera Bertam the park is about 5 minutes by car and about 3 km in a straight line. That is our own timing; peak-hour traffic is slower. InvestPenang puts the park about 23 km from the North Seberang Perai port and about 42 km from the airport.[5]

Setia Fontaines Industrial Park

A second industrial park is starting next door. Setia Fontaines Industrial Park covers 206 ha (about 509 acres) inside the 684 ha freehold Setia Fontaines township in Bertam, with ground broken in June 2026. It has light industrial, medium industrial and commercial plots for occupiers from small firms to large corporations, and targets advanced manufacturing, digital infrastructure, high-tech industry and low-carbon energy users. It has direct access to the North-South Expressway.[9]

Setia Fontaines township is about 6 minutes from Panthera Bertam. No job figure has been published for the industrial park yet.

Further afield: Kulim, Prai, Bayan Lepas and Gurun

  • Kulim Hi-Tech Park, Kedah, about 45 minutes. About 5,550 acres, with a workforce of 45,000 people and RM 179 billion invested from 1996 to 2024 (data as at April 2025).[11] Intel was its first tenant, in 1996, and its first four industrial phases, 2,161 acres, were fully leased by November 2024. Other investors named by MIDA include AT&S, Risen Solar, G-Crystal and Schott.[12] Infineon's two silicon carbide fab phases in Kulim, EUR 2 billion and EUR 5 billion, are set to create 1,500 jobs in Malaysia (Aug 2024).[13]
  • Prai Industrial Area and Free Zone, about 27 minutes, and Penang Port at North Butterworth Container Terminal, about 28 minutes. InvestPenang lists Perai Industrial Park among the state's industrial parks.[15]
  • Bayan Lepas Free Industrial Zone, on the island, about 40 minutes. InvestPenang calls Bayan Lepas the nation's first free-trade zone.[15]
  • Gurun, Kedah, approximately 37 minutes. Techvance Industries invested RM 50 million in an 11-acre factory in the Gurun Industrial Area, employing about 160 local staff and workers (Jun 2023).[14]

Across the state, Penang had RM 22.4 billion of approved manufacturing investment in 2025, up 29% on 2024, from 232 projects expected to create 24,633 jobs.[10] InvestPenang counts more than 350 multinationals and 6,500 small and medium firms in Penang.[15]

What kinds of jobs

Only what the sources say:

  • INV: described as skilled jobs (June 2025)[4] and as technical jobs (February 2026).[2][3] At the opening, the Chief Minister spoke of a local talent pipeline.[4]
  • Trensor: 200 high-skilled jobs for Malaysians (MIDA, May 2025).[6]
  • Sunshine PCB: more than 80% of the workforce projected to be Malaysian citizens (June 2026).[8]
  • The sectors enquiring at the park: semiconductors, E&E, automotive and automotive parts, new energy and energy storage (July 2026).[1] Back in June 2022, the Kepala Batas MP said industry in Bertam must be a high-technology area.[17]

None of the sources we found breaks these jobs down into engineers, technicians or operators, so we do not either.

For training, InvestPenang counts over 40 learning institutions in Penang, including four universities, more than 20 university colleges and five industrial training institutes.[15] The colleges and training institutes in Bertam itself are listed in our Living in Bertam guide.

Questions about working near Bertam

How far is Penang Technology Park @ Bertam from Panthera Bertam?

About 5 minutes by car, and about 3 km in a straight line. Peak-hour traffic is slower. The INV plant inside the park is about 6 minutes away.

Which companies are at Penang Technology Park @ Bertam?

INV New Material Technology has been operating its battery-separator plant since 28 June 2025. SuperSiC and Trensor were building their plants as of February 2026; in June 2026 Trensor's plant was reported as nearing completion, with no opening announced as of November 2026. Sunshine PCB had a plant planned as of June 2026, with no later update found as of November 2026. As of July 2026 more than 60% of the park's industrial land was taken up.

How many jobs are being created at PTP Bertam?

The Chief Minister said in June 2025 that INV's plant is expected to create 1,200 skilled jobs; reports in February 2026 put it at 1,500 technical jobs. MIDA says Trensor's plant will provide 200 high-skilled jobs for Malaysians. No total for the whole park has been published.

What kinds of jobs are they?

The reports describe them as skilled, technical or high-skilled jobs, in sectors such as semiconductors, electrical and electronics, automotive parts, new energy and energy storage. None of the sources we found breaks them down into engineers, technicians or operators.

Can I live in Bertam and work in Kulim, Prai or Bayan Lepas?

From Panthera Bertam it is about 27 minutes to the Prai Industrial Area, about 40 minutes to the Bayan Lepas Free Industrial Zone and about 45 minutes to Kulim Hi-Tech Park, by approximate drive times. Peak-hour traffic is slower.
