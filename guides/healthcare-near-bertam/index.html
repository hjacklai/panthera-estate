Hospitals and clinics near Bertam, Kepala Batas

The nearest hospital to Panthera Bertam is Hospital Kepala Batas, the government hospital, about 9 minutes by car. Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi USM, a cancer centre and teaching hospital in Bertam, is about 7 minutes, and a village clinic about 6. Private hospitals in Seberang Jaya, Butterworth, Bandar Perda and Sungai Petani are roughly 21 to 26 minutes away.

Hospital Kepala Batas

Hospital Kepala Batas is the Ministry of Health hospital for the town, on Jalan Bertam, 13200 Kepala Batas.[1] It is about 9 minutes by car from Panthera Bertam, 3.7 km in a straight line to the south-west.

  • Size. 134 beds, in the Penang State Health Department's 2024 health facts. The 2019 annual report gives the same figure.[1][2]
  • Specialist hospital. It is classed as a minor specialist hospital (hospital berpakar minor), one of four government hospitals in Penang offering specialist services in 2019.[2]
  • Part of a cluster. It belongs to the Seberang Perai hospital cluster, led by Hospital Seberang Jaya. Within the cluster it is designated the surgical expansion centre of excellence.[2]
  • Emergency department. It recorded 60,539 emergency department attendances and 40,452 specialist clinic attendances in 2019.[2]
  • No general outpatient department. The same report notes that Hospital Kepala Batas has no general outpatient department.[2] For a fever or a routine check-up, go to a government health clinic or a private clinic first.

Bed and attendance figures are from the dated official reports cited and may have changed. Check clinic times with the hospital before you go.

Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi USM (formerly AMDI)

Bertam has its own university hospital. The USM centre in Bertam formerly known as the Advanced Medical and Dental Institute (AMDI) was renamed the Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi Cancer Centre on 22 January 2026. Bernama describes it as a leading medical and dental centre in northern Peninsular Malaysia.[7]

  • Where. Bertam, 13200 Kepala Batas.[8] About 7 minutes by car and 1.4 km in a straight line from the estate.
  • What it does. It describes itself as the first teaching hospital dedicated to cancer. Its official site lists medical and dental specialist services and a clinical diagnostic laboratory among its facilities, and features news on its radiation therapy for cancer patients.[8]
  • Contact. General line +604-562 2888, from its official site.[8]

Clinics for everyday care

Because Hospital Kepala Batas has no general outpatient department, everyday GP-type care, such as a fever or a routine check-up, is at a Ministry of Health health clinic (klinik kesihatan), such as Klinik Kesihatan Tasek Gelugor, or at one of the other clinics nearby.

Across the district

In 2024, the Seberang Perai Utara district had 1 government hospital, 7 health clinics (klinik kesihatan), 20 village clinics (klinik desa), 3 Klinik 1Malaysia, 6 dental clinics and 4 dental specialist clinics. On the private side, the 2024 health facts list 121 private clinics, 36 private dental clinics and 12 private haemodialysis centres in the district, and no private hospital.[1] The 2019 annual report also listed community clinics in Bandar Putra Bertam and Pinang Tunggal, both in Kepala Batas; we could not confirm whether these still operate.[2]

Private hospitals within reach

The 2024 health facts list no private hospital in the Seberang Perai Utara district, which includes Bertam and Kepala Batas,[1] and as far as we could find in November 2026, none has opened there since (see What is planned). The nearest are about 21 to 26 minutes by car: south to Seberang Jaya, Butterworth and Bandar Perda, or north into Kedah to Sungai Petani.

Questions about hospitals near Bertam

What is the nearest hospital to Bertam?

Hospital Kepala Batas, the government hospital on Jalan Bertam, is about 9 minutes by car from Panthera Bertam. Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi USM, the cancer centre and teaching hospital formerly called AMDI, is about 7 minutes away.

Does Hospital Kepala Batas have an emergency department?

Yes. It recorded 60,539 emergency department attendances in 2019, according to the Penang State Health Department. It has no general outpatient department, so everyday GP care goes through the government health clinics and private clinics.

Is there a private hospital in Bertam?

Not yet, as far as we could find in November 2026. KMI Healthcare plans a 100-bed private hospital in Bertam, with completion targeted for 2028 (MIDA, March 2025; we found no later progress report as of November 2026). Today the nearest private hospitals are in Seberang Jaya, Butterworth, Bandar Perda and Sungai Petani, about 21 to 26 minutes away by approximate drive times.

Where can I get cancer treatment near Bertam?

Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi USM is in Bertam itself, about 7 minutes from the estate. It describes itself as the first teaching hospital dedicated to cancer. Sunway Medical Centre Penang in Seberang Jaya, about 21 minutes away, also offers radiotherapy.

Is Hospital Kepala Batas being expanded?

It is planned. In January 2026 the federal government approved funding for an initial study into an additional building at Hospital Kepala Batas, under the 13th Malaysia Plan. No cost, timeline or bed count has been announced.

How far are the larger hospitals?

Hospital Seberang Jaya, which leads the Seberang Perai hospital cluster and has 729 beds, is about 20 minutes away. Hospital Sultan Abdul Halim in Sungai Petani, Kedah, is about 27 minutes, and Hospital Pulau Pinang in George Town about 42 minutes, by approximate drive times.
