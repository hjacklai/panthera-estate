Guide · Getting around

Getting around from Bertam, Penang: roads, rail and buses

Panthera Bertam is about 7 minutes from the North-South Expressway at the Bertam Interchange, Exit 166. From the estate it is about 25 minutes to Butterworth town, 30 minutes to the Penang Bridge and 45 minutes to Penang International Airport, by approximate drive times. On the KTM Komuter station list for the Butterworth to Padang Besar route at its 2016 launch, the nearest station is Tasek Gelugor, and a May 2025 Bernama report lists Rapid Penang route 601 between Kepala Batas and Penang Sentral.

Updated 13 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

By car: the expressway and the local road

North-South Expressway, Exit 166

Bertam has its own junction on the North-South Expressway (Lebuhraya Utara-Selatan). OpenStreetMap tags it as the Bertam junction, number 166, signposted for Bertam and Kepala Batas.[2] It is about 7 minutes, 3.1 km west in a straight line, from Panthera Bertam.[1]

The expressway is run by PLUS; a PLUS media release of 14 May 2024 refers to Plaza Tol Bertam on the North-South Expressway.[3]

The local road

The main road in the area around the estate is Jalan Tun Hamdan Sheikh Tahir, state route P123. OpenStreetMap maps it as a primary road and, on the sections near the estate, a dual carriageway with two lanes each way.[4] Schools, shops and the hospital in Bertam and Kepala Batas are mostly under ten minutes away; the Living in Bertam guide lists them with times.

To Butterworth, the island and the airport

  • Butterworth town, about 25 minutes and 27 km by road, and Penang Sentral, about 28 minutes and 26 km.[1]
  • Penang Bridge, about 30 minutes and 25 km by road (home-page map figure).[1]
  • Second Penang Bridge (Jambatan Sultan Abdul Halim Mu'adzam Shah), about 37 minutes and 43 km to its mainland end at Batu Kawan, as a free-flow estimate.[12]
  • Penang International Airport, about 45 minutes and 44 km by road.[1]

Bridge and airport times are by car. Allow extra time at peak hours, and more for a flight.

North and east: Sungai Petani, Alor Setar and Kulim

Bertam is in northern Seberang Perai, and several Kedah towns are within reach. Approximate free-flow drive times from the estate:[12]

  • Sungai Petani town centre, about 19 km and 22 minutes.
  • Kulim town, about 29 km and 33 minutes. Kulim Hi-Tech Park is about 45 minutes on our home-page map.[1]
  • Alor Setar, about 83 km and 65 minutes.

Sungai Petani and Alor Setar are also on the KTM Komuter line from Tasek Gelugor (see below).

By train: KTM Komuter from Tasek Gelugor

The Komuter station list at the 2016 launch has no Bertam stop; the nearest is Tasek Gelugor.[5] It is about 11.5 km and 15 minutes by road from the estate (approximate, free-flow).[9]

Tasek Gelugor is one of the 13 stations on the KTM Komuter route between Butterworth and Padang Besar, launched on 1 January 2016.[5] KTMB, the national rail operator, lists two Komuter routes in the north in its route table: Butterworth to Padang Besar (157.8 km) and Ipoh to Butterworth; elsewhere on the same page it gives the second route as Ipoh to Bukit Mertajam.[6]

How often trains run

  • The Butterworth to Padang Besar route normally runs 38 services a day, counting both directions (The Star, August 2026).[8]
  • From Friday 24 July 2026 KTMB raised daily Komuter Northern Sector services from 56 to 62, and halved the evening-peak wait on the Padang Besar to Butterworth route to 30 minutes, from 60 (New Straits Times, July 2026).[7]

These reports cover the whole route and do not say how many trains stop at Tasek Gelugor. Check times with KTMB before you travel.

By bus: Rapid Penang

Rapid Penang routes serving Kepala Batas and Bertam: 601, 606 and 610 as reported by Bernama in May 2025,[10] and 612 as reported by The Star in January 2017.[11]

Routes and timetables change. Check with Rapid Penang before you travel.

Questions about getting around from Bertam

How far is Bertam from Butterworth?

From Panthera Bertam it is about 25 minutes to Butterworth town, about 27 km by road, and about 28 minutes to Penang Sentral. These are approximate drive times; peak-hour traffic is slower.

How long is the drive from Kepala Batas to Penang airport?

From Panthera Bertam, Penang International Airport is about 45 minutes and about 44 km by road, by approximate drive time.

Is there a KTM station in Bertam?

The KTM Komuter station list at the 2016 launch of the Butterworth to Padang Besar route has no Bertam stop. The nearest station on it is Tasek Gelugor, about 15 minutes from the estate by road (approximate).

Which expressway exit is Bertam?

Exit 166, the Bertam Interchange on the North-South Expressway, signposted for Bertam and Kepala Batas. It is about 7 minutes from Panthera Bertam.

Which bus goes from Penang Sentral to Kepala Batas?

A Bernama report of May 2025 lists Rapid Penang route 601 between Penang Sentral and Kepala Batas. We have not confirmed the current service, so check with Rapid Penang before you travel.

How far are Sungai Petani and Alor Setar from Bertam?

Sungai Petani town centre is about 22 minutes away and Alor Setar about 65 minutes. Both are approximate free-flow drive times from the estate.
