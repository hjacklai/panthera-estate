Panthera Bertam 3D walkthrough

A 3D model of each home type at Panthera Bertam, a freehold estate of 19 homes in Bertam, Penang, built from the floor plans. Scroll to raise the walls, then walk through every room.

The Semi-D

2,006 sq ft built-up, 4 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, freehold.

  • Walls: Built from the floor plan. Each home is 22 ft wide and 43.5 ft deep.
  • The pair: Two homes share a party wall. Each has its own gate, car porch and side patio.
  • Porch: 415 sq ft, for two cars, under the first-floor balcony.
  • Living: 321 sq ft, with a 3.5 m (11 ft 6 in) ceiling.
  • Patio: A 293 sq ft side patio, through sliding glass doors from the living room.
  • Dining room: 151 sq ft. Sliding glass doors to the side patio.
  • Dry kitchen: 128 sq ft, open to the dining room.
  • Wet kitchen: 78 sq ft, with a back door to the yard.
  • Guest bedroom: 145 sq ft, on the ground floor.
  • Bathroom: 50 sq ft. Doors from the guest bedroom and the wet kitchen.
  • Utility room: 57 sq ft, off the dry kitchen.
  • Stair: 21 risers of 176 mm, with a quarter landing.
  • Corridor: 80 sq ft. Leads to all three bedrooms.
  • Bedroom 1: 192 sq ft, with its own bathroom.
  • Bathroom: 40 sq ft, attached to Bedroom 1.
  • Bedroom 2: 156 sq ft, with its own bathroom.
  • Bathroom: 40 sq ft, attached to Bedroom 2.
  • Master bedroom: 337 sq ft, with a dressing area, its own bathroom and the front balcony.
  • Master bathroom: 80 sq ft.
  • Balcony: 105 sq ft, over the car porch.

The Bungalow

2,642 sq ft built-up, 4+1 bedrooms, 4+1 bathrooms (the +1 is the maid's room and its bathroom), freehold.

  • Walls: Built from the floor plan. The house is 46 ft wide and 28 ft deep.
  • The house: Five bedrooms on a 5,308 sq ft corner lot. Two pitched roofs with a flat roof between them.
  • Porch: 491 sq ft, for two cars, beside the entrance.
  • Entrance hall: 279 sq ft, double height, behind a timber double door.
  • Living: 276 sq ft. Sliding glass doors to the garden.
  • Guest bedroom: 138 sq ft, on the ground floor, with its own bathroom.
  • Guest bathroom: 40 sq ft, with a shower.
  • Powder room: 33 sq ft, off the entrance hall.
  • Dining room: 252 sq ft. Open to the wet kitchen, glass doors across the front.
  • Wet kitchen: 111 sq ft, with a breakfast counter and a back door.
  • Bathroom: 29 sq ft. Doors to the wet kitchen and the maid's room.
  • Maid's room: 62 sq ft, off the dining room.
  • Utility room: 76 sq ft, under the stair.
  • Corridor: 89 sq ft, overlooking the double-height hall.
  • Bedroom 1: 209 sq ft. Sliding doors to a 36 sq ft rear balcony.
  • Bathroom 1: 38 sq ft, attached to Bedroom 1.
  • Bedroom 2: 208 sq ft. Sliding doors to a 43 sq ft front balcony.
  • Bathroom 2: 42 sq ft, attached to Bedroom 2.
  • Master bedroom: 344 sq ft. Sliding doors to a 43 sq ft rear balcony.
  • Master bathroom: 114 sq ft, with a bathtub and a separate shower.
  • Balcony: 159 sq ft, over the porch.

The estate

  • The street: A 40 ft estate road with open drains and LED street lights on 9 m poles.
  • The estate: 19 homes on 1.96 acres: 16 Semi-Ds and 3 bungalows around a central green.
