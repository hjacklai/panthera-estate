Guide · Schools near Bertam

Schools near Bertam, Kepala Batas, Penang

Two government schools sit right beside Panthera Bertam: SK Bertam Perdana, a primary school about a 7-minute walk away, and SMK Bertam Perdana, a secondary school directly next to the estate. Within about 15 minutes there are more than twenty schools and colleges, including SM Sains Kepala Batas, MRSM, five Chinese and three Tamil primary schools, UiTM Kampus Bertam and Kolej Matrikulasi Pulau Pinang.

Right next door: SK and SMK Bertam Perdana

The school is a walk across the road. These are two separate government schools, one primary and one secondary, both in the Bertam Perdana township.

  • Sekolah Kebangsaan Bertam Perdana (SK, code PBA2038), Persiaran Bertam Perdana 2, 13200 Kepala Batas. A national primary school under PPD Seberang Perai Utara, running two sessions, morning and afternoon. The Ministry of Education's SekolahKu portal lists 1,776 pupils and 117 teachers.[1] About 300 m from the estate, a 7-minute walk.
  • Sekolah Menengah Kebangsaan Bertam Perdana (SMK, code PEA2092), Bertam Perdana 3, 13200 Kepala Batas. A national secondary school with a morning session only; SekolahKu lists 1,056 students and 67 teachers.[2] Its grounds sit directly beside the estate, about 0.5 km by road.[14]

Pupil and teacher numbers are the portal's figures, checked 13 November 2026. For places and enrolment, ask the school or the PPD Seberang Perai Utara district education office.

A closer look

  • SMK Dato' Kailan (PEA2058), Kepala Batas: a government secondary school with a morning session only, 920 students and 76 teachers on SekolahKu.[3]
  • SM Sains Kepala Batas (PEA2091), Jalan Tun Hamdan Sheikh Tahir: a Sekolah Berasrama Penuh, a fully residential science school, with 747 students and 70 teachers on SekolahKu.[4] About 5 minutes from the estate.
  • MRSM Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi: formerly MRSM Kepala Batas, renamed in honour of Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi in May 2025. The college began operating on 1 March 2007, took its first 359 students on 16 April 2007 and was officially launched on 29 March 2009.[10] OpenStreetMap still shows the old name.
  • SMK Bertam Putra (PEA2095), Jalan Seksyen 4, Putra Bertam: in the same area as SK Bertam Putra, with 139 students and 16 teachers on SekolahKu.[6]
  • SMK Bertam Indah (PEA2086), Taman Bertam Indah: 887 students and 60 teachers on SekolahKu.[5] It is not mapped on OpenStreetMap, so we have not given a distance.
  • Religious secondary schools (SABK). SekolahKu lists Madrasah Khairiah Islamiah (PFT2004) and Maahad Al-Imam An-Nawawi (PFT2003) in Kepala Batas, Penang. Neither is mapped on OpenStreetMap, so there is no distance here.[7]

University, pre-university and colleges

  • UiTM Cawangan Pulau Pinang Kampus Bertam, 13200 Kepala Batas. Bertam is a satellite campus of UiTM Cawangan Pulau Pinang; the branch's other campus is at Permatang Pauh.[11] About 1.0 km in a straight line (about 6 minutes' drive).*
  • Kolej Matrikulasi Pulau Pinang, Jalan Pongsu Seribu, 13200 Kepala Batas. It runs the Ministry of Education matriculation programme, a pre-university course.[12] About 13 minutes.
  • Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi, Universiti Sains Malaysia. A USM cancer centre at Bertam.[13] The map on our home page lists it as formerly AMDI. About 1.4 km in a straight line (about 7 minutes' drive).*

Teachers, lecturers and staff nearby

Schools and colleges are workplaces too. Questions about schools near Bertam

Which school is closest to Panthera Bertam?

SK Bertam Perdana, a national primary school about 300 m away, roughly a 7-minute walk across the road. SMK Bertam Perdana, the secondary school, sits directly next to the estate, about 0.5 km by road.

Is there a Chinese primary school near Bertam?

Yes. The nearest by road is SJK (C) Pei Yu in Kepala Batas (甲抛峇底培育华小), about 7.1 km and 9 to 10 minutes away. SJK (C) Nung Min and SJK (C) Sin Chung are each about 10 minutes, and SJK (C) Lee Chee and SJK (C) Mah Hua in Tasek Gelugor about 13 to 15 minutes.

Is there a Tamil school near Bertam?

Yes. SJK (T) Palaniandy on Jalan Kedah, Kepala Batas, is about 6.6 km by road, roughly 8 minutes. SJK (T) Ldg Malakoff is about 13 minutes and SJK (T) Ldg Mayfield about 15, both in Tasek Gelugor.

Is there a university or college in Bertam?

Yes. UiTM Cawangan Pulau Pinang Kampus Bertam is about 6 minutes from the estate. Kolej Matrikulasi Pulau Pinang, which runs the Ministry of Education matriculation programme, is about 13 minutes, and USM's Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi is about 7.

What is MRSM Kepala Batas called now?

MRSM Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi. The college was renamed in May 2025. It is about 3.2 km by road from Panthera Bertam, roughly 7 minutes.

Is SM Sains Kepala Batas near Bertam?

Yes, about 5 minutes away. It is a fully residential science school (SBP) on Jalan Tun Hamdan Sheikh Tahir, with 747 students listed on the Ministry of Education's SekolahKu portal.

Are the drive times exact?

No. They are approximate free-flow times from OpenStreetMap routing or from the map on our home page. Peak-hour traffic is slower.
