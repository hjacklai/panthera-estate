Guide · Semi-D or Bungalow

Semi-D or Bungalow: which suits your family?

The semi-D is designed as a first home that grows with your family: from RM 712,500, with a bedroom on the ground floor for parents that has its own door into a bathroom also reached from the living area, three bedrooms upstairs each with its own bathroom, a private side lawn on its open side and the shared green in front. Panthera Bertam has two home types. The semi-D is 2,006 sq ft on 210 to 318 m² of land, with 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms, from RM 712,500. The bungalow is 2,642 sq ft on 356 to 493 m², with 4+1 bedrooms and 5+1 bathrooms, from RM 1.22 million. Both are freehold, two storeys, have a bedroom on the ground floor and face the same shared green.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

Which suits your family

There is no single better choice; it depends on how many people live with you and how you use a home. What each one offers:

  • The Semi-D, a first home for a growing family. It is designed to grow with your family: a 145 sq ft bedroom on the ground floor for parents or guests, with its own door into a bathroom that is also reached from the living area, and three bedrooms upstairs, each with its own bathroom.[1] A big open-plan kitchen, living and dining, with full-height sliding glass doors that open the living and dining rooms onto your side lawn.[3] Every semi-D has a private side lawn on its open side (the other side is the shared party wall) and faces the shared green of over 700 m², and SK Bertam Perdana primary school is across the road, about a 7 min walk.[4] From RM 712,500.[1]
  • The Bungalow. The estate's biggest homes, with room for three generations. A ground-floor bedroom and bathroom mean parents or guests skip the stairs, with the rest upstairs.[2][3] It adds a maid's room with its own bathroom, a powder room for guests and a double-height entrance hall.[2] Sliding glass doors open the living room onto the rear and side garden and the dining room onto the patio.[2]

Budget 2027 proposes stamp duty relief for first homes priced up to RM750,000; it is a proposal, not yet law, as of 13 November 2026. See Budget 2027 first-home stamp duty.

Whichever you pick, it is yours to live in, or to rent out if you choose. For the factories and jobs around Bertam, see Living in Bertam.

Space and land

The bungalow has 636 sq ft more built-up than the semi-D: 2,642 sq ft against 2,006 sq ft.[1][2] It also sits on more land. The smallest bungalow lot, 356 m², is larger than the largest semi-D lot, 318 m².

  • Semi-D. 16 lots from 210 to 318 m² (2,260 to 3,423 sq ft). Every semi-D has the same 2,006 sq ft built-up; what changes from lot to lot is the land around it. Corner lots 04 and 19 carry a corner premium.[1]
  • Bungalow. 3 lots, 01 to 03, from 356 to 493 m² (3,832 to 5,308 sq ft). The house is the same on each; the land is not, so each bungalow is priced individually by its land size.[2]

Sales can send the live lot-by-lot list with sizes and availability.[1]

Bedrooms, bathrooms and floor heights

  • Semi-D: 4 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms. Each of the three bedrooms upstairs has its own bathroom; the ground-floor bedroom has its own door into the fourth bathroom, which is also reached from the living area.[1]
  • Bungalow: 4+1 bedrooms, 5+1 bathrooms. Four bedrooms plus a maid's room, and five bathrooms plus a powder room for guests. The guest bedroom and the maid's room on the ground floor each have their own bathroom, and so does every bedroom upstairs.[2]
  • Floor heights. Both homes have generous floor-to-floor heights of 3.7 m on the ground floor and 3.45 m upstairs, so rooms feel open and airy.[1][2] The Bungalow adds a double-height entrance hall.[2]
  • Sliding glass doors. In the Semi-D, full-height sliding glass doors open the living and dining rooms onto the side lawn and the master bedroom onto its front balcony.[1] In the Bungalow, they open the dining room onto the patio, the living room onto the rear and side garden, and all three bedrooms upstairs onto their own balconies.[2]

What both homes share

  • The green. Every one of the 19 homes faces a shared central green of over 700 m², kept as permanent public open space.[1][4]
  • Freehold, with its own title. The estate is on freehold land, Lot 24480, with an individual title per home at handover.[1][4]
  • Roads. A 40-foot inner ring road with two entrances runs around the green, and the layout keeps through-traffic out. The estate is not gated.[1]
  • Drainage. A 298 m² retention pond and perimeter drains are built in.[1]
  • Completion and handover. Estimated Q4 2028 for both.[4]

Questions about the semi-D and the bungalow

Which home suits a first-home buyer with children and parents?

The semi-D is designed as a first home that grows with your family. It is from RM 712,500, with a bedroom on the ground floor for parents or guests that has its own door into a bathroom also reached from the living area, three bedrooms upstairs each with its own bathroom, a private side lawn on its open side, and the shared green of over 700 m² in front. The bungalow has more space, 4+1 bedrooms and 5+1 bathrooms, with a ground-floor guest bedroom that has its own bathroom; it is from RM 1.22 million.

What is the difference between the semi-D and the bungalow at Panthera Bertam?

Size, land and layout. The semi-D has a 2,006 sq ft built-up on 210 to 318 m² of land, with 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms. The bungalow has a 2,642 sq ft built-up on 356 to 493 m², with 4+1 bedrooms (4 plus a maid's room) and 5+1 bathrooms (5 plus a powder room). Both are freehold, two storeys, and face the same shared green.

Which home has a bedroom on the ground floor?

Both. The semi-D has a 145 sq ft guest bedroom on the ground floor, for parents or guests, with its own door into a bathroom that is also reached from the living area. The bungalow has a 138 sq ft guest bedroom on the ground floor with its own bathroom.

How much land comes with each home?

The 16 semi-D lots run from 210 to 318 m² (2,260 to 3,423 sq ft). The 3 bungalow lots, 01 to 03, run from 356 to 493 m² (3,832 to 5,308 sq ft). The house is the same within each type; what changes is the land around it.

How much do the semi-D and the bungalow cost?

The semi-D is from RM 712,500 at pre-launch. The bungalow is from RM 1.22 million, and each of the three is priced by its land size. Prices are pre-launch, E. & O.E. Ask sales on WhatsApp for the figure for each lot.

Are both homes freehold?

Yes. The whole estate is on freehold land, and each home gets its own individual title at handover. Estimated completion and handover is Q4 2028.

Can I see inside both homes before I decide?

Yes. Both homes have a 3D walkthrough: the semi-D at /walkthrough/ and the bungalow at /walkthrough/?home=bungalow. Furniture, kitchen cabinetry, appliances and landscaping in the 3D are illustrative.
