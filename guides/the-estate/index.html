Panthera Bertam is a small freehold estate on the Penang mainland

Panthera Bertam is 19 two-storey homes, 16 semi-Ds and 3 bungalows, on 2 acres of freehold land in Bertam, Kepala Batas. The estate has a shared central green of over 700 m², kept as open space. A 40-foot ring road with two entrances runs around it. Pre-launch now, handover estimated for Q4 2028.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

The site is in Bertam, Seberang Perai Utara on the Penang mainland, at about 5.5307 N, 100.4655 E.[1] The developer is Panthera Development Sdn. Bhd.[1]

Homes around a green

The estate is a courtyard plan. At its heart is a shared green of over 700 m², a short walk from every front gate.[2]

  • The central green. Over 700 m² of shared space, kept as permanent public open space.[1]
  • The ring road. A 40-foot inner ring road around the green, with two ways in and out.[2]
  • Not gated. No gate, and the inner-ring layout keeps through-traffic out.[1][2]
  • Drainage built in. A 298 m² retention pond (kolam tadahan) and perimeter drains for quick diversion of storm water.[1][2]

Planting and landscaping in our renders and 3D walkthrough are illustrative.[1]

Two house types

All 19 homes are two-storey, modern tropical: pitched terracotta roofs, light walls, floor-to-ceiling sliding glass doors to the garden, and generous slab-to-slab heights of 3.7 m downstairs and 3.45 m upstairs.[1] Built-up from 2,006 to 2,642 sq ft.[2]

Land and title

The freehold site is 7,939.59 m² (1.962 acres, about 2 acres).[1] Each home comes with its own individual title at handover.[1]

Every lot is a different size: the 16 semi-D lots are 210 to 318 m², and the three bungalow lots, 01 to 03, are 356 to 493 m².[1] Ask sales for the lot-by-lot list with sizes and availability.[2]

What 19 homes means

Sixteen semi-Ds and three bungalows, and no more.[2] The inner ring road serves only these 19 homes.[1]

Extending and renovating

You can extend and renovate within the approved building envelope. A porch enclosure and a rear-kitchen extension are pre-approved. Changes to the streetscape need sign-off from MBSP, the Seberang Perai City Council, and you get a guideline at handover.[1][2]

Who is next door

  • SK Bertam Perdana, primary school across the road, about 300 m, a 7-minute walk.[3]
  • SMK Bertam Perdana, secondary school, nearby.[1]
  • Established terrace houses behind the site.[1]
  • Penang Technology Park @ Bertam, about 5 minutes by car.[1]

For schools, hospitals, shops, parks and drive times further out, see our Living in Bertam guide.

To live in, or to rent out

Live in it or rent it out, your choice. Penang Technology Park @ Bertam is about 5 minutes away, and more than 60% of its industrial land was taken up as of July 2026.[1] Our Living in Bertam guide lists the companies building there and nearby, with dates and sources.

Questions about the estate

What is Panthera Bertam?

A pre-launch freehold estate of 19 two-storey homes in Bertam, Kepala Batas, on the Penang mainland: 16 semi-detached homes and 3 bungalows around a shared green of over 700 m², developed by Panthera Development Sdn. Bhd.

Is Panthera Bertam freehold?

Yes. The whole 2-acre site is freehold, and each home comes with its own individual title at handover.

Is Panthera Bertam a gated estate?

No. The homes sit on a 40-foot inner ring road with two ways in and out, and the layout keeps through-traffic out.

When will the homes be ready?

Handover is estimated for Q4 2028. Pre-launch registration is open in 2026.

Can I see the homes before they are built?

Yes. Our 3D walkthrough, built from the floor plans, takes you through each home and out to the street and the estate. Furniture and landscaping are illustrative.

Can I rent my home out?

Yes. Live in it or rent it out, your choice. Penang Technology Park @ Bertam is about 5 minutes away; our Living in Bertam guide lists the companies building there.
