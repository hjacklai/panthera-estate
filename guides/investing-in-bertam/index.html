Guide · Investing in Bertam

Investing in Bertam, Penang: the facts

Bertam, in Kepala Batas on the Penang mainland, sits next to Penang Technology Park @ Bertam, an 880-acre industrial park that had all its infrastructure complete and more than 60% of its industrial land taken up as of July 2026. Named investors there include INV New Material Technology (RM 6.4 billion) and Trensor Electronics (RM 100 million), and S P Setia broke ground on a 509-acre industrial park in Bertam in June 2026. Official data show the district, Seberang Perai Utara, had 868 residential transactions in Q3 2026 (preliminary) and an estimated 354,700 people in 2026.

Updated 13 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

Jobs and investment arriving

Penang Technology Park @ Bertam is an 880-acre industrial park in Seberang Perai Utara, developed by Ideal Property Group[1][2] and launched in March 2023.[3] Works listed in February 2026 included upgrading the main access road to a four-lane dual carriageway, and water, power, natural gas and 5G links.[2] In July 2026 the developer said all its infrastructure works were complete and more than 60% of the industrial land had been taken up.[1] The park is about 5 minutes from Panthera Bertam.

Across the border in Kedah

Kulim Hi-Tech Park (KHTP) is in Kedah.[15] Infineon opened Phase 1 of its silicon carbide power fab there on 8 August 2024, a EUR 2 billion (RM 9.8 billion) investment with 900 jobs in Phase 1.[14] AT&S opened the first phase of its RM 8.5 billion IC substrate campus there in January 2024, with about 2,500 employees targeted for that phase.[15]

Housing stock and new supply

Seberang Perai Utara had 98,540 existing homes at Q3 2026, about 75.5% of them landed. It completed 2,702 new homes from January to September 2026, the most of Penang's five districts (6,307 statewide), and had 3,847 more in the incoming supply.[21]

Industrial property

Industrial property transactions in the district: 21 (RM 104.98 million) in Q3 2025, 58 (RM 246.49 million) in Q2 2026 and 41 (RM 200.81 million) in Q3 2026 (preliminary).[19]

Roads, rail and what is planned

In place

  • Rail. The KTM Komuter Butterworth to Padang Besar line, running since 2016, stops at Tasek Gelugor in Seberang Perai Utara.[24] From 24 July 2026 KTMB raised Komuter Northern Sector services from 56 to 62 a day, and evening peak waits on the Padang Besar to Butterworth route fell to 30 minutes from 60.[25]

Planned or under way

Plans can change or slip. Each one is labelled with its status and the date of the report.

  • Package 1 under construction (Sep 2026); full project targeted for 2030 (Apr 2026). The traffic dispersal project between the Juru and Sungai Dua toll plazas on the North-South Expressway, in the Perai and Bukit Mertajam area, including a 17.4 km highway to separate through traffic from local traffic (Mar 2025).[22][33] Package 1, an upgrade of the East-West Interchange roundabout and a new flyover at Jalan Tun Hussein Onn, began on 21 October 2025.[23] It was 40.79% complete in September 2026, with completion expected in October 2027; Packages 2 to 5 were at the evaluation and tendering stage.[34]
  • Awaiting gazettement, targeted for October 2026 (Jul 2026); gazettement not confirmed as of 13 Nov 2026. The Seberang Perai Local Plan 2030, whose public exhibition has been completed.[28] Its 2021 draft named Kepala Batas-Bertam one of three catalyst hubs, planned as the main administrative centre for Seberang Perai Utara, with Kepala Batas and Bertam to be developed into a commercial centre for high-tech industries.[30] Across the three hubs together, the draft proposed high-tech parks, logistics hubs and transit-oriented development.[29]
  • Proposed, completion targeted Q1 2028 (Mar 2025). A 100-bed hospital in Bertam with a RM 146 million gross development cost, under a build-and-lease deal between Bertam Specialist Hospital Sdn Bhd and TDM unit KMI Bertam Medical Centre.[27][35]
  • Study approved (Jan 2026). An initial study for an additional block at Kepala Batas Hospital; no cost or timeline yet.[26]
  • Listed in Budget 2026 (Oct 2025). An upgrade of the Bertam Sports Complex; KWAP's RM 300 million commitment to senior housing in Kepala Batas; and the Sungai Nyiur flood mitigation project in Seberang Perai Utara.[32]
  • Proposed in a draft; no funding or timeline given in the report (Aug 2026). A Butterworth to Kulim railway line, one of 14 high-impact projects in the draft review of the Penang State Structure Plan 2040.[31]

Questions about Bertam

What is Penang Technology Park @ Bertam?

An 880-acre industrial park in Seberang Perai Utara, developed by Ideal Property Group. In July 2026 the developer said all its infrastructure works were complete and more than 60% of the industrial land had been taken up. It is about 5 minutes from Panthera Bertam.

Which companies are building factories in Bertam?

At Penang Technology Park @ Bertam: INV New Material Technology, whose RM 6.4 billion battery separator plant opened in June 2025; Trensor Electronics, which broke ground on a RM 100 million plant in May 2025; and SuperSiC Malaysia, which broke ground on a silicon carbide wafer plant in July 2025. Sunshine PCB has presented plans for a circuit board plant there. Also in Bertam, S P Setia broke ground on the 509-acre Setia Fontaines Industrial Park in June 2026.

How much manufacturing investment did Penang approve?

RM 22.4 billion in 2025, from 232 projects expected to create 24,633 jobs. From January to June 2026 it was RM 17.3 billion from 177 projects, the most of any state.

How many people live in Seberang Perai Utara?

339,095 in the 2020 census. The Department of Statistics Malaysia estimates 354,700 for 2026.

How many homes are sold in Seberang Perai Utara?

NAPIC recorded 868 residential transactions worth RM 338.65 million in Q3 2026 (preliminary), about 21% of all residential transactions in Penang that quarter.
