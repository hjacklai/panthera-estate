Kepala Batas, Penang: the town in brief

Kepala Batas is a town on the Penang mainland and the seat of the Seberang Perai Utara district, postcode 13200. Bertam, where Panthera Bertam is, is its fast-developing side. It is not Kepala Batas in Kedah, and Bertam here is not Bertam in Melaka.

Updated 13 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

Where Kepala Batas is

The chain, smallest first: Bertam, Kepala Batas, Seberang Perai Utara (SPU, northern Seberang Perai, also spelt Seberang Prai), Penang mainland, Pulau Pinang. Postcode 13200.

Kepala Batas is the seat of the Seberang Perai Utara district, and its local council is the Seberang Perai City Council (MBSP).[1][12] The district covers about 269 km² and had 339,095 people in the 2020 census.[4][5] Bertam is close to the North-South Expressway,[6] and Panthera Bertam is about 8 minutes' drive from the town centre (approximate, from the map on our home page).

Not Kepala Batas, Kedah. Not Bertam, Melaka.

Two of the names here are shared with other places, which trips up searches and maps:

  • Kepala Batas, Kedah is a separate town in the Kubang Pasu district, where Sultan Abdul Halim Airport is.[2]
  • Bertam, Melaka is a separate area in the Melaka Tengah district, at the other end of the west coast.[3]

When you search or set a map, add "Penang" or "Pulau Pinang", or use the postcode 13200.

The name and a little history

The name is Malay: kepala is the head or front end of something, and batas the earth dykes around a paddy field. Paddy farming was the main work on this muddy plain.[1]

Malay settlements grew here in the 19th century, and the town stayed rural, rice and small trade, through British rule. Today it is an administrative, education and cultural centre for northern Seberang Perai, known for Islamic and pondok education, and it is the hometown of Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi, Malaysia's 5th Prime Minister.[7]

East of town, much of the land was rubber. Bertam House, built in 1913 as the Bertam Estate manager's bungalow, still stands; the estate land later became the Bertam Perdana township.[8]

How to get to Kepala Batas

  • By car. Take the North-South Expressway to the Bertam Interchange, Exit 166.[9] From Panthera Bertam, Butterworth town is about 25 minutes and the Penang Bridge about 30 (approximate).
  • By bus. Rapid Penang route 601 runs between Penang Sentral and Kepala Batas.[1]
  • By train. The nearest station is Tasek Gelugor, on the KTM Komuter Padang Besar to Butterworth line.[10]

The names in Malay and Chinese

  • Bahasa Malaysia. Kepala Batas, Seberang Perai Utara, Pulau Pinang. Mainland Penang is Seberang Perai, sometimes called Tanah Besar.
  • Chinese. Kepala Batas is 甲抛峇底,[11] Seberang Perai Utara is 威北 and Seberang Perai is 威省. On this site Bertam stays in Latin script.

Questions about Kepala Batas

Is Kepala Batas in Penang or Kedah?

There is one in each. Kepala Batas, Penang is on the Penang mainland, the seat of the Seberang Perai Utara district, postcode 13200. Kepala Batas, Kedah is a separate town in the Kubang Pasu district, where Sultan Abdul Halim Airport is.

How far is Kepala Batas from Butterworth?

From Panthera Bertam, Butterworth town is about 25 minutes by car and the Penang Bridge about 30 minutes, approximate drive times outside peak hours.

How do I get to Kepala Batas by public transport?

Rapid Penang route 601 runs between Penang Sentral and Kepala Batas. The nearest train station is Tasek Gelugor, on the KTM Komuter line.

Is Bertam in Penang the same as Bertam in Melaka?

No. Bertam in Penang is part of Kepala Batas on the Penang mainland. Bertam in Melaka is a separate area in the Melaka Tengah district.
