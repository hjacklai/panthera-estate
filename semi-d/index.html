The Semi-D: a first home in Bertam, Penang, designed to grow with your family

The Semi-D at Panthera Bertam is a freehold first home designed to grow with your family: room for the children upstairs, where each of the three bedrooms has its own bathroom, a bedroom on the ground floor for parents or guests, with its own door into a bathroom that is also reached from the living area, a private side lawn on its open side, and a shared green of over 700 m² in front. It is a two-storey semi-detached home in Bertam, Kepala Batas, Seberang Perai Utara, on the Penang mainland, with 2,006 sq ft built up on 210 to 318 m² of land, 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms. It is in pre-launch now, from RM 712,500.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

A first home for the whole family

The Semi-D is designed as a first home a family can grow into: children now, and parents who may come to live with you later. It is a freehold semi-D in Penang from RM 712,500, a starting price below RM750,000, with an individual title at handover.

  • Parents on the ground floor. A 145 sq ft bedroom downstairs, for parents, grandparents or guests, with its own door into a bathroom, which is also reached from the living area, so parents need not use the stairs.
  • Children upstairs. The 337 sq ft master bedroom with a front balcony, and bedrooms of 192 and 156 sq ft. Each of the three has its own bathroom.
  • A private side lawn. Every Semi-D has a private lawn on its open side; the other side is the party wall it shares with its neighbour.
  • The green in front. Every home faces the shared green of over 700 m², kept as permanent public open space, and the estate layout keeps through-traffic out.
  • Schools next door. SK and SMK Bertam Perdana are next to the estate. SK Bertam Perdana primary school is across the road, about a 7 min walk. More in Schools near Bertam.

The green it faces

All 19 homes sit around a shared green of over 700 m², kept as permanent public open space. It is a short walk from every front gate.

A 40-foot inner ring road with two entrances runs around it, and the layout keeps through-traffic out. The estate is not gated. A 298 m² retention pond and perimeter drains are built in. A Residents' Association looks after the green, roads, drains and pond from handover, at around RM 180 per home a month.

Semi-D questions

Does the Semi-D suit a family with children and parents?

It is designed to grow with your family. There is a 145 sq ft bedroom on the ground floor for parents or guests, with its own door into a bathroom, which is also reached from the living area, so parents need not use the stairs. Upstairs are three bedrooms, each with its own bathroom. Every Semi-D has a private side lawn on its open side (the other side is the shared party wall) and faces the shared green of over 700 m², and SK Bertam Perdana primary school is across the road, about a 7 min walk.

Does the Semi-D have a garden?

Yes. Every Semi-D has a private side lawn on its open side (the other side is the shared party wall), and the front faces the shared green of over 700 m², kept as permanent public open space.

Is the Semi-D freehold?

Yes. The estate is on freehold land, Lot 24480, and each home gets its own individual title at handover.

How much is the Semi-D?

From RM 712,500 at pre-launch. Corner lots 04 and 19 carry a premium. Ask sales on WhatsApp for the live lot-by-lot list.

How many bedrooms does the Semi-D have?

Four bedrooms and four bathrooms. Upstairs, each of the three bedrooms has its own bathroom. On the ground floor, the fourth bedroom has its own door into the fourth bathroom, which is also reached from the living area.

When will the Semi-D be ready?

Estimated completion is Q4 2028.
