Living in Bertam, Penang, near Penang Technology Park

Bertam is a town in Kepala Batas, on the Penang mainland, about 24 km by road north of Butterworth. Penang Technology Park @ Bertam is a few minutes' drive away, and schools, shops and a public hospital are within about ten minutes. Panthera Bertam, a 19-home freehold estate, sits here, across the road from SK Bertam Perdana.

Updated 12 November 2026 · Panthera Development Sdn Bhd

Where Bertam is

The full address chain: Bertam, Kepala Batas, Seberang Perai Utara (SPU, northern Seberang Perai, also spelt Seberang Prai), on the Penang mainland, Pulau Pinang. The estate is Lot 24480, at about 5.5307 N, 100.4655 E, postcode 13200.

Two names here are shared with other places. This is Kepala Batas in Penang, not Kepala Batas in Kedah, and Bertam in Penang, not Bertam in Melaka. The Kepala Batas guide covers the difference.

Sungai Petani in Kedah is about 19 km north by road, and Butterworth and the Penang Bridge are to the south. Homes on the mainland cost less than on the island, which is a big part of why people look here.[11]

Jobs: Penang Technology Park @ Bertam

  • Penang Technology Park @ Bertam (PTP Bertam). 880 acres of industrial land, launched in 2023. By July 2026 all its infrastructure was complete and more than 60% of the industrial land was taken up.[1] It is about five to six minutes from the estate by our own estimate.
  • INV New Material Technology. An RM 6.4 billion battery-separator plant inside the park, built in two phases. Phase 1 started on 28 June 2025, and full operation is targeted for June 2027.[2]
  • Setia Fontaines Industrial Park. 509 acres in Bertam; ground was broken on 20 June 2026.[3]
  • Penang as a whole. RM 22.4 billion of manufacturing investment was approved in 2025, across 232 projects expected to create 24,633 jobs.[4]

Further out, by the site's own estimates: Prai Industrial Area about 27 minutes, Bayan Lepas Free Industrial Zone about 40 minutes and Kulim Hi-Tech Park about 45 minutes.

Getting around: highway and rail

The North-South Expressway (PLUS) has an interchange at Bertam, a short drive from the estate, which is the quick way south to Butterworth, Penang Sentral and the Penang Bridge, or north to Sungai Petani and Alor Setar.

The nearest railway station is KTM Tasek Gelugor, about 12 km by road, with KTM Komuter and ETS services.[6] There is no station in Bertam itself. Penang Sentral in Butterworth, about 26 km away, is the hub for buses, KTM trains and the ferry to the island.

What is planned

Plans change, so treat these as plans, not facts on the ground:

  • INV aims for full operation of its plant in June 2027.[2]
  • KMI Healthcare's 100-bed Bertam hospital is targeted for 2028.[9]
  • The Seberang Perai 2030 local plan was targeted for gazette in October 2026; check its status before relying on it.[10]

Questions about Bertam

How far is Panthera Bertam from Penang Technology Park @ Bertam?

About five to six minutes by car, by our own estimate. The park covers 880 acres, and by July 2026 more than 60% of its industrial land was taken up.

How far is Bertam from Butterworth?

About 24 km by road, roughly 25 minutes without traffic. Penang Sentral is about 26 km away and the Penang Bridge about 28 km.

Is there a train station near Bertam?

The nearest is KTM Tasek Gelugor, about 12 km by road, with KTM Komuter and ETS services. There is no station in Bertam itself.

Which schools are near the estate?

SK Bertam Perdana, a primary school, is across the road, and SMK Bertam Perdana, a secondary school, is close by. UiTM Kampus Bertam is about 3 minutes away.

Is there a hospital nearby?

Hospital Kepala Batas, the public hospital, is about 7 km away, around 9 minutes. Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi USM (formerly AMDI) is about 4 minutes away. A 100-bed private hospital in Bertam is planned for 2028.
